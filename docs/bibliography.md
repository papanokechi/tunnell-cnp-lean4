# Bibliography

## Primary references

1. **Tunnell, J. B.** (1983). "A classical Diophantine problem and modular forms of weight 3/2."
   *Inventiones Mathematicae*, 72(2), 323–334.
   DOI: [10.1007/BF01389327](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01389327)
   *Source of Tunnell's theorem linking congruent numbers to ternary quadratic form counts.*

2. **Koblitz, N.** (1993). *Introduction to Elliptic Curves and Modular Forms*. 2nd ed.
   Springer GTM 97.
   *Chapter IV covers congruent numbers in detail.*

3. **Silverman, J. H.** (2009). *The Arithmetic of Elliptic Curves*. 2nd ed. Springer GTM 106.
   DOI: [10.1007/978-0-387-09494-6](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-0-387-09494-6)

4. **Birch, B. J. & Swinnerton-Dyer, H. P. F.** (1965). "Notes on elliptic curves. II."
   *J. reine angew. Math.*, 218, 79–108.
   *Statement of the BSD conjecture used in Tunnell's conditional direction.*

## Specific theorems used

- **Tunnell's Theorem** (ref 1): characterization of congruent numbers via quadratic forms.
- **BSD Conjecture** (ref 4): the reverse direction of Tunnell's criterion
  requires that L(E, 1) = 0 implies rank(E) ≥ 1 for the relevant curves.
- **Mordell–Weil Theorem**: E_n(ℚ) is finitely generated; rank + torsion determines
  rational point structure.
